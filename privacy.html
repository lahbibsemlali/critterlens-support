Privacy Policy

How your photographs and data are handled

Effective October 9, 2026. CritterLens identifies insects and spiders from photographs. No CritterLens account is required. Camera and photo access are requested when you choose to capture or import an image; the app does not browse your library in the background.

Before each new photograph is analyzed, the review screen asks you to agree to share it. A resized JPEG copy is sent over HTTPS to our Supabase backend and then to OpenAI for AI identification. Image dimensions are included; the app does not request your GPS location. Avoid submitting photographs containing people, addresses, or other personal information. You can decline by returning without tapping Agree & identify.

The identification function does not save uploaded photographs or results in Supabase Storage or a cloud journal. OpenAI requests use store=false. This does not mean zero retention: OpenAI may retain content in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days by default, or longer when legally required or reasonably necessary to protect services or others from harm, with additional safety exceptions for images. API content is not used for model training by default. Supabase retains technical and security request logs that include IP addresses, approximate location derived from IP addresses (such as country, region, city and postal code), client technical information and connection fingerprints, request timestamps, status codes and function execution timings. These logs support operation, security and troubleshooting of the identification service. Log retention depends on provider policies and project settings. The app does not request GPS location.

Successful identifications and their photographs save to History automatically on your device. Favorites, appearance settings, and naturalist preferences are also stored locally. iOS device backups may include this data depending on your backup settings. You can delete observations and their saved photographs in History or clear all history in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes its local data; deleting history does not cancel a subscription or erase providers' server records.

The online field guide sends the species names you search for, selected categories, and requested species IDs to iNaturalist. Reference photographs load from iNaturalist and its image hosts, which receive your IP address and technical request information. iNaturalist may retain search and reference requests with IP and technical information and use these records to operate its service and understand usage, as described in its privacy policy at https://www.inaturalist.org/pages/privacy. These requests do not include your identification photographs, subscription identifiers, or GPS location. Catalogue pages, reference images, and bookmarked species may be cached on your device. Clear Catalogue Cache in Settings removes cached catalogue pages; remove a species bookmark to delete its separately saved reference entry. Source links open external websites under their own privacy policies.

The app creates a random installation identifier. Supabase stores a SHA-256 hash of it with daily scan counters and budget reservations to enforce usage limits. This identifier is also used as your RevenueCat app user ID. When purchases are configured, RevenueCat and Apple process purchase records, receipts, subscription status, and technical information needed to provide and restore Pro access. RevenueCat also uses purchase records to provide subscription analytics, such as purchase history and revenue reporting. Payment-card details are handled by Apple and are not received by CritterLens.

The app has no advertising SDK, does not request an advertising identifier, and does not share data for cross-app advertising tracking. We do not sell your data. Photos and subscription identifiers are shared with service providers for app functionality, not public posting.

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send. Do not send sensitive personal information. For questions or requests concerning provider-held data, use the Support & Feedback page and include your RevenueCat support ID if relevant. Provider retention and any applicable legal obligations may affect deletion. Manage subscription cancellation separately through your Apple account.

Questions or privacy requests? Contact support.
