Support & Feedback

For app questions, identification feedback, or privacy requests, open a support request on GitHub. A GitHub account is needed to post a request. You can also read existing answers in our support issues.

GitHub issues are public. Do not post receipts, purchase identifiers, addresses, personal photos, passwords, or API keys. For a privacy request, ask for private contact instructions without including personal details.

Common questions

Photo identification requires internet access and consent on the review screen. Try a close, well-lit photo with the whole specimen visible. Results are estimates; do not use them to decide whether an animal is safe to handle.

The free plan allows one successful identification per UTC day; Pro allows up to 100. A service pause can also result from the shared scan budget. Clear history in Settings to delete locally saved observations and photographs.

To restore an eligible subscription, use Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple account. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account settings. For refunds, use Apple purchase support. Deleting the app or history does not cancel a subscription.
