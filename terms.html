Nature exploration & safety guidelines

CritterLens is an educational insect and spider identification utility. Use of the app is subject to Apple's Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement, available through the link below, and these service disclosures.

Automated identifications are estimates and can be incorrect. Photograph quality and unresolved distinguishing features affect results. Do not rely on the app to determine whether a specimen is safe, diagnose a bite, select treatment, or make pest-control decisions. Do not touch, handle, or consume unknown insects or spiders. Seek appropriate professional medical help after a concerning bite or sting.

Photo identification requires internet access and your agreement to send the selected photograph through Supabase to OpenAI. Submit only images you have permission to use. The bundled field guide and previously saved local observations can be viewed offline.

The free plan includes one successful identification per installation per UTC day. CritterLens Pro includes up to 100 successful identifications per UTC day. Failed or non-identifiable scans do not count as successful identifications. The field guide and local journal are available on both plans. Shared service-budget and availability limits may temporarily pause identification. Do not automate requests or attempt to bypass usage limits.

Available weekly and yearly subscriptions show localized App Store prices before purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation. Subscriptions automatically renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends; your account is charged for renewal within the last 24 hours of the period. Manage or cancel through your App Store account settings. Restore Purchases recovers eligible purchases using your Apple account. No free trial is promised by the app.

Subscriptions do not provide cloud backup. Saved photographs and observations remain in local app storage and may be included in your device backups. Deleting local history or uninstalling the app does not cancel your subscription. For purchase support and other questions, use Support & Feedback.
